FAA knowledge test prep

Regulations cited are 14 CFR Parts 61 and 91, NTSB Part 830, and the AIM, as of 2026. Passing score on the FAA test is 70%: 42 of 60 questions in 2.5 hours.

Progress is saved as you go. On the Progress tab you can copy a progress code to carry it to another device.

Shortcuts: Space shows the answer, 1 missed, 2 got it. In a test, 1 2 3 pick an answer and Enter moves on.
